ABOUT THE ADVOCATE

Experience guided by principle.

Advocate Mmati Essie Mashilo is an Advocate of the High Court of South Africa, a member of the Pretoria Society of Advocates, and holds Chambers at New Court Chambers.

She holds an LLB from the University of South Africa (UNISA) and has developed practical experience across various areas of law through legal practice and professional experience.

Her professional background has given her a strong foundation in legal research, legal writing, case analysis, drafting, negotiation and client consultation.
